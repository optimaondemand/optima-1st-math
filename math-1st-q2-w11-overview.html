Essential Question
Our Big Question
🤔
Think About This…
“How can we create our own stories to show what happens when we subtract?”
🎯
Objectives
I Can Statements
1
I can create a story that matches a subtraction problem.
2
I can use words like “took away,” “left,” or “gave away” to tell a subtraction story.
3
I can solve the subtraction problem in my own story.

Before You Begin
Notice & Wonder

Think about having 8 crayons and giving 3 of them away to a friend. What do you notice about the words you would use to tell this as a story? What do you wonder about how someone else’s subtraction story might be different from yours, even if the numbers are the same?
