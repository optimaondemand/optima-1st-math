Essential Question
Our Big Question
🤔
Think About This…
“How can we use what we know about numbers to 20 to put them in order and tell which is greater or less?”
🎯
Objectives
I Can Statements
1
I can count, read, and write numbers to 20.
2
I can put numbers to 20 in order from least to greatest and greatest to least.
3
I can use a number line or objects to show how I know one number is greater or less than another.

Less Than

A number that is smaller or fewer. For example, 8 is less than 15.

Before You Begin
Notice & Wonder

What do you notice about where a bigger number like 17 would land compared to a smaller number like 12 when you count in order? What do you wonder about how you could tell which of two numbers is greater without counting all the way from the beginning?
