Before You Begin
Notice & Wonder

Think about having 9 crayons in one box and 4 crayons in another, and moving 1 crayon from the second box to fill up the first box to 10. What do you notice about how the numbers change, and why the problem now looks like 10 and some more? What do you wonder about whether making a ten would help with other problems, like 8 + 5 or 7 + 6?
