Objectives
I Can Statements
1
I can name positions using ordinal words like first, second, third, fourth, and fifth, up to tenth.
2
I can tell the difference between a number that tells how many (5) and an ordinal number that tells position (fifth).
3
I can write and say ordinal numbers to explain where something is.

Vocabulary Words

Ordinal Number
tap to flip
A special number word that tells the position or order of something, like first, second, or third.
📍
Position
tap to flip
The place where something is in a line or group, such as first in line or third from the left.
👀
Before You Begin
Notice & Wonder

Think about standing in line, where you are fourth and your friend is right behind you. What do you notice about how the words first, second, third, and fourth tell exactly where each person is standing? What do you wonder about what your friend’s position would be if the whole line turned around and faced the other way?
