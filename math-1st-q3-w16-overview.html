Essential Question
Our Big Question
🤔
Think About This…
“How can subtracting the ones first to get to 10 help us solve subtraction problems within 20?”
🎯
Objectives
I Can Statements
1
I can subtract a number from 10 and find what is left.
2

3

Before You Begin
Notice & Wonder

Think about having 14 crayons and needing to give away 6, so you first give away the 4 extra ones to leave exactly 10, and then give away 2 more from the 10. What do you notice about why stopping at 10 makes the rest of the problem easier? What do you wonder about whether this trick would also work for problems like 15 − 7 or 13 − 5?
