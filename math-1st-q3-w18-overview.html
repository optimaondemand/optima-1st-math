Essential Question
Our Big Question
🤔
Think About This…
“How can we tell flat shapes from solid shapes, sort them by what is the same about them, and put shapes together to make new ones?”
🎯
Objectives
I Can Statements
1
I can name flat shapes, like circles, squares, rectangles, triangles, and hexagons, and solid shapes, like cubes, cones, cylinders, and spheres.
2
I can group shapes by what they have in common, like number of sides, corners, or flat faces.
3
I can put shapes together to make a new shape, like two triangles making a rectangle.

Key Terms
Vocabulary Words

Hover or tap to flip!

📄
Flat Shape
tap to flip
A shape that lies flat on paper, like a circle, square, triangle, or hexagon. It has length and width but no thickness.
📦
Solid Shape
tap to flip
A shape you can hold and pick up, like a cube, sphere, cone, or cylinder. It takes up space in every direction.
👀
Before You Begin
Notice & Wonder

Think about a can of soup, and then think about the circle you would see if you looked at just the top of the can. What do you notice about how the flat circle is part of the solid can? What do you wonder about what new shape you could make if you put two triangles together along one side?
