Before You Begin
Notice & Wonder

Think about starting with a group of 9 objects and splitting them into two smaller groups, like 5 in one hand and 4 in the other. What do you notice about how the two smaller groups relate back to the number 9 you started with? What do you wonder about whether there are other ways to split 9 into two different groups?
