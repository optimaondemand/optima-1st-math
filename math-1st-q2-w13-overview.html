Before You Begin
Notice & Wonder

Think about starting with 10 and adding 3 more ones to get 13, and then taking away 2 ones to get 11. What do you notice about how the ten stays the same while the ones change? What do you wonder about what would happen to the number if you added a whole new ten instead of just a few ones?
