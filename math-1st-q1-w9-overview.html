Before You Begin
Notice & Wonder

Think about starting with 8 crackers, eating 3 of them, and then counting back from 8 to find out how many are left. What do you notice about where you land when you count back 3 numbers from 8? What do you wonder about whether taking away objects one at a time gives you the same answer as counting back?
